BP Texas City Refinery Explosion

1974 · Texas City, TX · 15 fatalities, 180 injuries

Overfilled distillation tower during startup released a vapor cloud that ignited near occupied contractor trailers sited too close to the process unit. Root cause: instrumentation failure, poor facility siting, deferred maintenance.
